Questions

Before you apply

Do I need AI experience?

Many roles don't ask for any prior AI experience. Your professional judgement is the skill, and each listing sets out its own requirements.

Who do I apply to?

The hiring company. Every link takes you straight to the company's own application page, so you apply to them, not to us.

Does it cost me anything?

No. You never pay Rekrootly anything. We may receive a referral fee from the hiring company if you're engaged.

Who decides whether I'm engaged?

The hiring company. Roles are contract work with them, and they agree the terms with you directly.
